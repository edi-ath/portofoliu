About Elara

Based inCopenhagen, Denmark
Available forWorldwide Travel

I'm Elara — a photographer obsessed with the quiet tension between stillness and motion, and the way light transforms the ordinary into something profound.

My work spans editorial, portrait, wedding, and landscape photography. Each genre shares a common thread: I believe the most honest photographs happen when the subject forgets the camera exists entirely.

I've had the privilege of shooting across fifteen countries, from the fjords of Norway to the salt flats of Bolivia. What drives me isn't the destination but the in-between — the moments before and after the "shot."

When I'm not behind the lens, you'll find me developing film in my darkroom, walking coastal paths, or cooking slow food for anyone who'll sit still long enough.

"A photograph is a secret about a secret. The more it tells you, the less you know." — Diane Arbus
